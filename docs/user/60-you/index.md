# You

Your account is a passkey, recovery words for when you lose it, and the browsers, devices and tools signed in as you.

## What is here

- **Passkey.** The key pair on your device that signs you in. See [Passkey](/docs/reference/keys/#passkey).
- **Recovery.** Twenty-four words that get you back in when you lose your passkeys. See [Recovery words](/docs/reference/keys/#recovery-words).
- **Browsers.** How long a browser stays signed in. See [Browser sign-in](/docs/reference/keys/#browser-sign-in).
- **Apps.** The keys that let the command line and AI tools act as you. See [CLI sign-in API key](/docs/reference/keys/#cli-sign-in-api-key).
