feat(shim): retain a borrowed frame while queue credit is exhausted (#129)

Add a prefix-consuming decoder callback that may defer its current complete
frame without copying its payload. Preserve the existing eager decoder as
a wrapper; the caller owns the unused coalesced suffix and retries retained
frames before reading more bytes (D128, D215).

Reject buffering decoded owned payloads in a second queue: that duplicates
up to 1 MiB per peer and complicates ordering. Returning the exact consumed
prefix preserves control/input order and the existing bounded frame parser.
Clarify the existing shared-writer capability includes queue deferral
independently of an ordinary writer selector; no wire bit is added.

Validation: isolated baseline frame slice formatting and strict shim
all-target clippy passed; frame suite passed 9 tests with 1 ignored; output/source hashes are recorded
in evidence. The regression test changes follow as a separate test slice.

Refs: #129
