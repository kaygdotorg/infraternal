fix(agent): negotiate queue credit for ordinary native writers (#129)

An ordinary native terminal can reach the surviving shim full input queue
without negotiating its existing bounded deferral capability. Request the
shared-writer capability when offered while retaining the ordinary stable
writer selector and the legacy capability intersection (D128, D215).

sharing is independent of an ordinary terminal requesting queue credit.
No new capability or foundational dependency is introduced.
