Keep capability-negotiated native fallback writers warm before switching

When live mesh and enrolled fallback support primed writers, park the
fallback with prime intent, validate its cached capabilities, send client
Hello once and start its owned native supervisor before control activation.
Keep this supervisor across switching so existing shim writers need no new
TLS or ordinary-writer replacement. TCP and legacy standby remain unchanged.

The transport owner captures its retirement guard before spawning and
aborts children when dropped. A regression showed abort-before-first-poll
leaked the connection when the guard was created inside the async body;
the captured owner fixes that cancellation case. Detached tasks, another
handshake at failover and global stream-mode promotion were rejected.
