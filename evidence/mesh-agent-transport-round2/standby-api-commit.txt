Declare negotiated support for parked enrolled fallback transports (#129)

Expose one additive channel capability for servers that can park an
already ledger-admitted enrolled fallback without replacing the live host.
Clients can keep the fallback authenticated while mesh is active and reuse
its greeting on activation. Legacy peers remain on their existing path.
