Add per-stream priming to the common native terminal relay

A standby writer must query durable input state without displacing the
ordinary writer or reading output ahead of its cursor. Negotiate native
priming capability, then require canonical transport.prime before that
stream's AttachTerm. Unmarked writers continue ordinary attachment even
after the enclosing connection is promoted.

Use the shared-writer shim helper, send its authoritative Ack before any
input, and move only the shadow output cursor before existing credit can
fund reads. Keep the same relay, retained-input/reconnect behavior and
bounded command queue. Connection-wide mutable priming mode was rejected:
a binding crossing promotion could otherwise steal the ordinary writer.
The exported relay entry point states its supervisor authority invariant.
Decisions198,215 preserve native-only additive negotiation and TCP v1.
