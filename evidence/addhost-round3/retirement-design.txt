Retirement intent is inserted atomically with invitation/address metadata. Cancellation cuts the existing host guard before awaiting the database; an owned writer drains transport writes and commits tombstone plus intent deletion together. Caller cancellation does not cancel that writer. Startup retires all remaining intents before transport listeners. Existing hosts have no intent. Completion removes intent only after readiness and installation commit. Failed completion keeps the intent and must report failure, rather than silently reporting Done with authority that startup will retire.
