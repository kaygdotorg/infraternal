fix(server): retain native packet identity across cancelled actor reads

Actor cancellation could discard a dequeued native packet while waiting for
the writer map, and stale queued Close/Ack could affect a rebound writer.
Keep pending ownership inside Incoming and validate the originating Arc before
delivery; publish the binding before starting its reader. Release the delivery
map during network setup, rechecking under the binding lock. Share RemoteLink
ownership so bounded native flights can be introduced independently. Reject
blind stream-id matching and holding the map across QUIC backpressure.
Decisions D64, D128, D198, D215; no priming capability change in this slice.

TLS/QUIC runtime suite: 28 passed, 0 failed, 3 ignored. Raw output in
